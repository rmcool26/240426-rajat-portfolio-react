## 1. Operation Comfort Control

### What the project was
Operation Comfort Control is a commercial equipment repair/service website. The project came as an assessment where the existing website already existed, but the goal was to improve usability, trust, and lead generation.

### What was happening
The existing website needed better UX clarity and stronger conversion flow. Visitors needed to quickly understand the service, trust the company, and take action without confusion.

### What Rajat did
Rajat redesigned the website in two versions:

- **Version 1:** A practical UX-fix version that improved the existing website with minimal creative deviation.
- **Version 2:** A more polished and creative redesign that still respected UX fundamentals and business goals.

The second version was preferred because it balanced better visual quality with clearer service communication.

### Why it matters
This project shows Rajat’s ability to handle real-world redesign constraints: improve an existing website without breaking the business intent, then push it further when the client asks for a stronger visual direction.

### Outcome
The redesign helped improve lead generation, with a reported **5% lead increase in the last quarter**.

## 7. IIDE

### What the project was
IIDE is an education and digital business institute with multiple web properties including the main site, careers, learning platform, blogs, resources, and lead-focused course pages.

### What was happening
The websites were already live on WordPress and generating leads, but after COVID recovery the business needed stronger lead-page redesigns, reusable components, faster updates, better tracking, and smoother operations.

### What Rajat did
Rajat redesigned lead-focused pages, created global header/footer/course card systems, automated course deadline updates, managed interns/junior developers, and helped push production-ready pages live. He also worked deeply with LearnDash, Gravity Forms, Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Integromat/Make, and Freshsales.

He also solved a complex WordPress/jQuery use case where a lead form’s terms link could open the correct course accordion on a separate terms page using the course name as the key.

### Why it matters
This project shows Rajat’s practical product-design plus implementation ability: not just UI screens, but live WordPress systems, automation, tracking, team management, and conversion improvements.

### Outcome
The redesign and operational improvements contributed to a reported **16% yearly increase** across course pages.
